FOR USE WITH
MONGOOSE 2E

EVERY ROLL

2D + characteristic DM + skill against a target number. That is the whole game.

EFFECT — HOW WELL, NOT JUST WHETHER

EffectResult
6 or moreExceptional success
1 to 5Average success
0Marginal success
−1Marginal failure
−2 to −5Average failure
−6 or lessExceptional failure
Effect = your roll − the target number. It adds to damage, sets duration, and carries into the next check in a chain: +2 exceptional, +1 average, 0 marginal, then −1, −2, −3 the other way.

BOONS & BANES

A boon rolls three dice and keeps the best two. A bane keeps the worst two. One of each cancels out. Reach for these instead of inventing a modifier.

YOUR SHIP

Jump fuel = 10% of hull tonnage per parsec. A jump takes about a week either way.
Jumping inside the hundred-diameter limit is DM−4 and invites a misjump.
Reaching that limit under thrust takes hours — which is the whole reason piracy works.

THE CODE OF THE STARS

Take cargo and leave crews alive and no great power will spend a fleet on you.
Kill crews, take ships or hit convoys and you become a problem worth solving.
Raid the same ground twice and the next freighter is a Q-ship.

The letter of marque protects nobody — not until Drinax is restored. Then every crime retroactively becomes the lawful service of a privateer.

WHAT YOU ARE OWED

King Oleb takes 10% off the top of everything.
Then shares: captain 5, each Traveller 2, each crewman 1.
Divided only in a safe harbour, once the cargo is sold. Every month without a division costs the crew 1 Morale, and at 0 they mutiny.

Short range is within a quarter of the weapon's Range. Long is beyond Range but under twice it. In close combat only one-handed weapons fire; rifles are clubs. Move while locked in melee and your enemy gets a free swing at DM+2.

HITS & HARM

Damage = the weapon's dice + Effect. Armour subtracts from every hit.
It falls on END first; when END is gone it comes off STR or DEX, your choice. One physical characteristic at zero and you are down. Two means a serious wound needing surgery.

CLOSE COMBAT

Neither of you may attack anyone else while locked together.
Only one-handed weapons fire; a pistol can be parried aside.
Rifles and anything larger are used as clubs.
Break away and your enemy gets an immediate free attack at DM+2.

WHEN IT RUNS LONG

Some tasks take more than a round. Roll the timeframe, and keep working.
Take damage mid-task and you must make the skill check again immediately, with the damage as a negative DM. Fail and this round's work is wasted; fail by −6 and start again from nothing.

SPACE COMBAT AT A GLANCE

FOR USE WITH
MONGOOSE 2E

THE ROUND six minutes

StepWhat happens
INITIATIVEonce per battle 2D + Pilot skill + Thrust. Rolled once for the ship, not per person. Better pilots and faster drives act sooner.
1 · MANOEUVRE In initiative order, every ship allocates its Thrust between moving and combat manoeuvring. All of it is done by the pilot.
2 · ATTACK Once every ship has moved, they shoot. 2D + Gunner skill + DEX DM, and the range band sets the difficulty.
3 · ACTIONS Everything else — repairs, sensors, boarding.

RANGE AND WHAT IT COSTS

BandDistance Thrust
Adjacent1km or less1
Close1–10km1
Short11–1,250km2
Medium1,251–10,000km5
Long10,001–25,000km10
Very Long25,001–50,000km25
Distantover 50,000km50
That is the cost to change band by one step. A thrust-2 ship cannot cross Medium in a round however badly she wants to — which is the decision the whole fight turns on. Most fights begin at Very Long or Distant and close to Long or Medium before anyone can shoot.

COMBAT MANOEUVRES one Thrust each

AID GUNNERS Pilot check. A task chain to your gunners — a steadier platform along the attack vector.
DOCKING Pilot check. Opposed, at DM−2, if they do not want you. Boarding follows a success.
LINE UP A SHOTA better firing solution.
EVASIVE ACTION Every other manoeuvre is once a round. This one repeats.

YOUR DUTY pick one at the start

DutyWhat you can do
PILOT Movement and every combat manoeuvre. Evasive action — each point of unspent Thrust dodges one attack, at a negative DM equal to your Pilot skill.
TURRET GUNNER One turret, chosen at the start. Point defence against a missile salvo, once a round: Gunner (turret) check, and the Effect removes that many missiles. Two lasers give DM+1, three give DM+2. A weapon used for point defence cannot attack that round. Disperse sand against lasers. Reload.
BAY GUNNEROne bay weapon.
ENGINEER Power plant, drives, damage control. Repair a system.
SENSOR OPERATOR Sensor lock for your gunners. Electronic warfare against theirs.
COMMANDER Improve initiative for the ship. Leadership and Tactics.
MARINERepel boarders, or board them.
PASSENGERWait in your stateroom and hope.
Reassignment is an action anyone can take: move to another duty. A ship's computer can cover the pilot, the engineer, the sensor operator or a gunner if it is running the software for it — which is how four people fly a ship built for eight.

WHEN YOU ARE HIT

Damage falls on the hull. Armour subtracts from every hit before the hull feels it. At zero she is dead in space — drifting, not destroyed, which is what makes a prize worth taking rather than a wreck.

TAKING A PRIZE

FOR USE WITH
MONGOOSE 2E

THE FIVE STEPS

1Intercept her.
2Attack, or stop her running.
3Board — optional, and where the money usually is.
4Loot her.
5Escape.

1 · INTERCEPT

You must not only get in range — you must match velocity, or there is nothing to board. She can dodge, but every evasion adds to her own time to destination.
IF YOU ARE FASTER Higher Thrust than hers and inside 100,000km: escape is very difficult indeed.
AT SHORT OR LESS Opposed Pilot checks as she jinks and dodges.
THE CLOCK Starts the moment you open fire. Somebody heard it.

2 · STOP HER

CALLED SHOTS At Short or less, direct-fire only — no missiles or torpedoes. Nominate the location, then roll.
AIM FOR The manoeuvre drive, which cuts her Thrust and makes the intercept easy. Or the fuel, dumping it into space so she cannot jump away.
DEMAND SURRENDER Average (8+) Electronics (comms), 1D×10 seconds, SOC. Subtract her current Morale.
CARE Breach the hull and you risk destroying the cargo you came for.
Surrenders are rarely unconditional. She will want safe passage for ship and crew in exchange for part of the cargo — and many merchants keep a few tons of sacrificial cargo aboard exactly for this.

3 · BOARD

DOCK, CRIPPLED Routine (6+) Pilot, 1D minutes, DEX.
BREACHING CHARGE Average (8+) Explosives, 1D minutes, EDU.
MOVING IN ZERO-G Average (8+) DEX, 1D seconds.
FIND THE HOLD Average (8+) Recon, 1D minutes, INT.
Nobody resists boarders without vacc suits on, or the internal doors shut — a breach at the airlock or hold will otherwise vent the bridge. Assume both sides are suited. The weapons that matter are cutlasses, snub pistols and anything else that works in zero gravity and does not hole the hull.

4 · LOOT

SHIP'S LOCKER Very Difficult (12+) Electronics (computers), 1D×10 minutes, EDU.
THE RIGHT CARGO Average (8+) Recon, 1D minutes, INT.
SHIFTING A CONTAINERby hand Very Difficult (12+) STR, 1D minutes.
Hunt the valuable things first. Leave what you cannot safely take. Pirates have been left aboard a crippled merchant by a captain who would rather run than fight for them.

5 · ESCAPE

You still have to reach the hundred-diameter jump limit. The common mistake is chasing a target too far into a planet's jump shadow and finding you cannot leave — jumping inside the limit is DM−4.

USING A POWER

Roll the talent's skill + your PSI DM against the power's difficulty.
Spend the PSI cost on a success, 1 point on a failure.
Going below zero PSI applies the excess as damage. At 0 PSI you cannot try at all.
Recover 1 point per hour, starting three hours after you last used a talent.

CARRYING SOMEONE ELSE'S BURDEN

Awareness only ever works on yourself. Only a Zhodani psion can push it onto someone else, and only if that person is not behind a psionic shield.

The same is true of teleportation: you move yourself, and never armour, loose objects or another person — unless you have the Zhodani Teleprojection ability.

TRAINING

PSI = 2D − terms served. Testing Cr5000, two weeks.
Training four months, Cr100000. Roll PSI check per talent, DM−1 for each attempt already made.
Telepathy +4 · Clairvoyance +3 · Telekinesis +2 · Awareness +1 · Teleportation +0
Take Telepathy first and it is gained automatically. Learned talents start at level 0.

KIT AND COUNTER-KIT

Psi-drugs (TL8) restore PSI; failure means 3D damage and −1 PSI permanently.
Inhibitor drug (TL9, Cr500) DM−3 to all PSI checks and no recovery. END check each hour to shake it.
Psionic shield (TL12, Cr4000) in a helmet or hood. Blocks reading, probing and assault.
Psionic interface (+20%) use PSI DM instead of DEX DM on a device.
In the Imperium, psionics are illegal and the institutes are underground.

WHAT OTHERS NOTICE

Telepathy is felt by other telepaths — they always know when their emotions are being bent.
Clairvoyance is undetectable, even by other psions.
A shielded mind cannot be read or probed, and turns an assault into a duel of opposed checks.

HOW TO USE THIS SHEET

FOR USE WITH
MONGOOSE 2E

THE BASICS

Pale blue fields calculate themselves. Current, DM, totals, encumbrance, armour value, UPP — fill in the white fields and the blue ones follow. You never type into them.

Override in the header unlocks them anyway and turns them amber, for when the rules or your referee decided something the arithmetic disagrees with. Switch it off and the formulas take over again.

? Help turns on tap-to-explain. Touch any field and a bar at the bottom of the screen says what it is for.

CHARACTERISTICS

Base is who you are — it only changes with ageing, training or permanent injury.
Misc is anything temporary or artificial: combat drugs, a cybernetic limb, a field dressing.
Injury is damage you are currently carrying.
Current = Base + Misc − Injury. DM comes from Current, plus anything in DM Mod.

In play, damage lands on END first. When END reaches zero it spills into STR or DEX — your choice. One physical characteristic at zero and you are down; two means a serious wound needing surgery.

SKILLS

Lvl is the skill level from your career. Misc is a bonus from gear or circumstance. Tot adds them. Train is a box to tick while you are studying something.

A skill you do not have is rolled at DM−3 — or DM−1 if you have Jack-of-All-Trades. Blank lines with a rule under them are for you to write in the specialty: Art, Language, Science, Profession.

COMBAT

Weapons. Fill in the damage from the book, and the magazine as now / max so you can count rounds. Atk DM is your skill plus the relevant characteristic DM — work it out once and write it down.

Armor. Tick Worn for what you actually have on; only ticked rows count toward the total. Armour subtracts from every hit that lands.

Encumbrance. Carried weight adds up your gear, weapons and worn armour. You are encumbered once it reaches STR + END + Athletics.

SAVING & SYNC

The sheet saves as you type — watch the indicator at the top right.

Add to Home Screen only after your character has loaded — if the address bar has no ?id= in it, the sheet is not attached to anything and nothing will be saved. A red bar across the bottom will tell you if that ever happens.

Your link is your character. Open the same link on a phone, a laptop or a tablet and you get the same sheet — it lives on the campaign server, not in one browser. Nothing to export, nothing to carry across.

Leave it open in two places and both stay in step — type on the laptop and the phone updates a moment later. A field you are actively typing in is never overwritten underneath you, so there is nothing to manage.

Two characters? Open each link in its own tab. Each tab stays the character its link points at.

If your referee gave you a link, this sheet is tied to that character and lives on the campaign server. The header shows live · campaign when it is connected. Your edits reach the referee's console; the wounds and spent ammunition they apply appear here, with a brief note that the referee changed something.

Switch changes to a different character in the same campaign.
⇩ Files saves a copy to your device, and ⇧ Load reads one back. Keep one after each session — it is your insurance if the network is down.

Print gives you the paper sheet with everything filled in, on US Letter. This How To page does not print. If your printer dialog offers a scale setting, leave it on 100% or Auto — the sheet already leaves itself room.
