HOW TO USE THIS SHEET

FOR USE WITH
MONGOOSE 2E

THE BASICS

Pale blue fields calculate themselves. Current, DM, totals, encumbrance, armour value, UPP — fill in the white fields and the blue ones follow. You never type into them.

Override in the header unlocks them anyway and turns them amber, for when the rules or your referee decided something the arithmetic disagrees with. Switch it off and the formulas take over again.

? Help turns on tap-to-explain. Touch any field and a bar at the bottom of the screen says what it is for.

CHARACTERISTICS

Base is who you are — it only changes with ageing, training or permanent injury.
Misc is anything temporary or artificial: combat drugs, a cybernetic limb, a field dressing.
Injury is damage you are currently carrying.
Current = Base + Misc − Injury. DM comes from Current, plus anything in DM Mod.

In play, damage lands on END first. When END reaches zero it spills into STR or DEX — your choice. One physical characteristic at zero and you are down; two means a serious wound needing surgery.

SKILLS

Lvl is the skill level from your career. Misc is a bonus from gear or circumstance. Tot adds them. Train is a box to tick while you are studying something.

A skill you do not have is rolled at DM−3 — or DM−1 if you have Jack-of-All-Trades. Blank lines with a rule under them are for you to write in the specialty: Art, Language, Science, Profession.

COMBAT

Weapons. Fill in the damage from the book, and the magazine as now / max so you can count rounds. Atk DM is your skill plus the relevant characteristic DM — work it out once and write it down.

Armor. Tick Worn for what you actually have on; only ticked rows count toward the total. Armour subtracts from every hit that lands.

Encumbrance. Carried weight adds up your gear, weapons and worn armour. You are encumbered once it reaches STR + END + Athletics.

SAVING & SYNC

The sheet saves as you type — watch the indicator at the top right.

Your link is your character. Open the same link on a phone, a laptop or a tablet and you get the same sheet — it lives on the campaign server, not in one browser. Nothing to export, nothing to carry across.

Leave it open in two places and both stay in step — type on the laptop and the phone updates a moment later. A field you are actively typing in is never overwritten underneath you, so there is nothing to manage.

Two characters? Open each link in its own tab. Each tab stays the character its link points at.

If your referee gave you a link, this sheet is tied to that character and lives on the campaign server. The header shows live · campaign when it is connected. Your edits reach the referee's console; the wounds and spent ammunition they apply appear here, with a brief note that the referee changed something.

Switch changes to a different character in the same campaign.
⇩ Files saves a copy to your device, and ⇧ Load reads one back. Keep one after each session — it is your insurance if the network is down.

Print gives you the seven-page paper sheet with everything filled in. This How To page does not print.
